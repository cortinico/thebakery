Balloon with Jaewoong Eum

Elegant Android UI libraries and much more with Jaewoong Eum

Jaewoong Eum · 25 Oct 2022 · 33:24
Also on Spotify Apple Podcasts Pocket Casts RSS

Elegant Android UI libraries are the mastery of today’s guest! Please welcome Jaewoong Eum on stage to talk about Balloon 🎈.

Balloon is a library to display tooltips on Android apps. Display tooltips might sound easy, but it turns out that there is much complexity under the hood.

Jaewoong will walk us through this project, and will share his experience as a sole maintainer of this and other popular Android libraries.

Enjoy the show 👨‍🍳
