How much of your coding agent can you make your own? Today we're recording **in person** during the GDE Summit, with [**Iury Souza**](https://iurysouza.dev/), a mobile developer and platform engineer who loves tinkering with his dev tools.

Iury takes us through his agentic development setup with [**OpenCode**](https://opencode.ai/) and [**Pi**](https://pi.dev/), from choosing models for planning and implementation to teaching an agent to build its own extensions. We explore what a harness actually does, how skills and `AGENTS.md` shape the context a model sees, and why sharing your perfect setup can be just as tricky as sharing your dotfiles.

We also talk about the future of terminal interfaces, the reality of running local models on a laptop, and how to keep up when agents produce more work than you can comfortably review. Iury shares his experiments with annotated plans and his open-source [Visual Artifact Renderer](https://github.com/iurysza/visual-artifact-renderer), which turns agent output into visual explanations, code reviews, and dashboards.

Enjoy the show 👨‍🍳
