About

Welcome to the bakery

The Developers' Bakery is a podcast about developer tooling and the people who build and use it. Every episode asks the same question in a different way.

What are developers using to bake today's software, and what did they learn along the way?The question behind every episode

The formats

Deep Dive

A guest, a story

Conversations with someone building or using developer tools, built around one story.

Fresh from the Oven

The three of us

Just the hosts, on a topic we can't stop talking about: news, setups, hot takes, conference recaps.

Recipe of the Week

Many episodes end with each of us sharing one small tool or trick. Episodes are recorded remotely and, whenever we can, in person at conferences and meetups.

The bakers

Your hosts

Nicola Corti

Founder · Android GDE

Nicola started the Developers' Bakery in 2020 and hosted the first 99 episodes on his own. He's an Android GDE and works on Android infrastructure for React Native at Meta, based in London. He has founded and run developer communities across Europe, and in his free time he bakes real bread.

GitHubXncorti.com

Marco Gomiero

Co-host since #100 · Android GDE

Marco was the guest of episode 52 and is now on the other side of the microphone. He's a software engineer at Airalo and a Google Developer Expert for Android, and he builds apps and the agent tooling that builds them, like FeedFlow and Difftray. Ask him about his agentic setup at your own risk.

GitHubXmarcogomiero.com

Paolo Rotolo

Co-host since #100

Paolo is a regular at developer conferences around Europe, where most of the conversations that led to Season 2 happened. He brings the questions the other two forgot to ask.

GitHubXrotolo.dev
