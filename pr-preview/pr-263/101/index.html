#101Deep Dive

Making coding agents your own with Iury Souza

Custom harnesses, local models, and better ways to collaborate with coding agents

Iury Souza · 10 Sep 2026 · 21:29
Also on Spotify Apple Podcasts Pocket Casts RSS

How much of your coding agent can you make your own? Today we’re recording in person during the GDE Summit, with Iury Souza, a mobile developer and platform engineer who loves tinkering with his dev tools.

Iury takes us through his agentic development setup with OpenCode and Pi, from choosing models for planning and implementation to teaching an agent to build its own extensions. We explore what a harness actually does, how skills and AGENTS.md shape the context a model sees, and why sharing your perfect setup can be just as tricky as sharing your dotfiles.

We also talk about the future of terminal interfaces, the reality of running local models on a laptop, and how to keep up when agents produce more work than you can comfortably review. Iury shares his experiments with annotated plans and his open-source Visual Artifact Renderer, which turns agent output into visual explanations, code reviews, and dashboards.

Enjoy the show 👨‍🍳
