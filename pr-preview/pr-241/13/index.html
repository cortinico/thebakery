Apollo GraphQL with Martin Bonnin

Martin is on stage to tell us about GraphQL and Apollo Android, the most popular GraphQL client for Android

Martin Bonnin · 14 Jun 2021 · 43:24
Also on Spotify Apple Podcasts Pocket Casts RSS

Today we have on stage Martin Bonnin, Android Engineer at Apollo GraphQL 🚀.

Martin has the amazing opportunity to work full time for an open-source project: Apollo Android, probably the most popular GraphQL client for Android.

In this episode Martin will tell us his experience with GraphQL, how he got involved with Apollo and how it is to work full time on a oss project.

Enjoy the show 👨‍🍳
