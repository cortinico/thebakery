WithThe three hostsLength20–50 min

Recurring segment

Recipe of the Week

At the end of an episode, each of us brings one small thing that made our week easier: a CLI, a config trick, a hidden-gem library. Links in the show notes.

  • MarcoArchifyTurns plain descriptions or Mermaid into polished architecture diagrams.#102
  • NicolaZencastrRemote recording with a separate track per speaker.#102
  • PaoloGitHub stacked pull requestsSplit a big change into reviewable layers, natively.#102
  • NicolaVowenFree, fully local dictation for prompts, emails and chats.#100
  • MarcoLet old PR comments update your AGENTS.mdAsk your agent which review comments the instructions should have prevented.#100
