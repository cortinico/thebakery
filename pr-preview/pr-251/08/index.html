Tuist with Pedro Piñera Buendía

Today we have Pedro on stage to tell us how to build iOS projects with Tuist!

Pedro Piñera Buendía · 15 Mar 2021 · 45:11
Also on Spotify Apple Podcasts Pocket Casts RSS

You don’t need a tooling team

That’s Tuist motto, and if you’re an iOS Developer 🍎 you really don’t want to miss it.

Today we have Pedro Piñera Buendía on stage. He’s the author and maintainer of Tuist, a tool to help you maintain and generate your XCode Projects. Pedro will tell us about the history of this tool and how it plays with monorepo and dependency management.

Enjoy the show 👨‍🍳
