Be a guest

Come on the show

We're always looking for Deep Dive guests. You don't need to maintain an open source project. You're a good fit if you:

  • built a developer tool, big or small, open source or not
  • changed how your team builds, tests, reviews or ships
  • have a setup other developers would want to steal
  • organise something for developers: a conference, a community, a meetup

Just want to say hi or suggest a topic for Fresh from the Oven? Same form, or write to {{ site.links.email }}.
